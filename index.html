Caring for those who rest, and for the families who remain.

The Malacca Christian Cemetery Board looks after Christian cemeteries and burials in Malacca. If you are arranging a burial or have a question about a grave, we are here to help.

Who we are

The Malacca Christian Cemetery Board (MCCB) is a local charity. We look after cemetery grounds and support Christian families through the practical side of burial, so that you can focus on saying goodbye.

We keep our work simple and our manner gentle. Ask us anything, at any hour of the week. The secretary will reply as soon as possible.

How we can help

  • Arranging a burial

    Talk to us about dates, plots, and what is needed on the day.

  • Questions about a grave

    Find a family grave, check records, or ask about visiting.

  • Care of the grounds

    Ask about upkeep of the cemetery and how the community can support it.

When you message us

It helps the secretary reply faster if you can share a few details. If you do not have them all, send what you have.

  • Your name and a phone number we can reach you on
  • The name of the person who has passed away, if applicable
  • The cemetery or grave you are asking about, if you know it
  • Your preferred date, if you are planning a burial
